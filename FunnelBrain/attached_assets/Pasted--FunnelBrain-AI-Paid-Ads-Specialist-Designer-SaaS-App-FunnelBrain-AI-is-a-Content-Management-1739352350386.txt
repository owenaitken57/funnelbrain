# FunnelBrain AI – Paid Ads Specialist & Designer SaaS App

FunnelBrain AI is a **Content Management System (CMS) SaaS app** designed to serve as a paid media specialist and designer. It empowers marketers to create, manage, and optimize paid ads across multiple channels (LinkedIn, Google Ads, X Ads, Instagram, and Facebook) with a strong emphasis on design and performance. The app integrates AI to generate full-funnel marketing strategies and provides actionable insights to improve ad performance.

---

## App Flow and Features

### **1. Landing Page**
When users open the web app, they land on a **modern and sleek landing page** that includes:
- **Overview**: A brief description of FunnelBrain AI’s features and benefits.
- **Pricing**: Details about the **Free Version** and **Plus Version**.
  - **Free Version**: Limited ad creations, basic analytics, and access to a few templates.
  - **Plus Version**: Unlimited ad creations, advanced analytics, AI-powered strategy generation, and custom templates.
- **Sign-Up Button**: Users can sign up for either plan, which redirects them to the **main dashboard**.

---

### **2. Main Dashboard**
After signing up, users are taken to the **main dashboard**, which serves as the central hub for all activities. The dashboard includes:

#### **a. Toolbar (Sidebar)**
The toolbar on the left side provides quick access to key features:
- **Create Ads**: Opens the ad creation tool.
- **Ad Analytics**: Displays performance data for ads.
- **AI Strategy**: Generates full-funnel marketing strategies.
- **Settings**: Manage account details, integrations, and preferences.

#### **b. Ad Creation Tool (Center)**
The center of the dashboard is dedicated to **creating ads**. This tool is similar to Canva but more modern and sleek, with features like:
- **Templates**: Pre-designed templates for different ad channels and funnel stages.
- **Custom Designs**: Users can upload their own SVGs or design files to create ads.
- **Ad Variants**: Automatically generates ads in all required sizes for LinkedIn, Google Ads, X Ads, Instagram, and Facebook.
- **Drag-and-Drop Interface**: Intuitive design tools for customizing ads (fonts, colors, images, etc.).

#### **c. Ad Analytics**
Clicking on the **Ad Analytics** tab opens a new page that displays:
- **Performance Data**: Fetches data from connected ad accounts (via API) to show metrics like CTR, CPC, conversions, and ROI.
- **Week-on-Week Stats**: A breakdown of PPC KPIs with filters to view specific data (e.g., by channel, date range, or campaign).
- **Insights**: Highlights which creatives are working and which aren’t, with actionable recommendations.

#### **d. AI Strategy Generator**
The **AI Strategy** tab integrates with **DeepSeek or ChatGPT** to:
- Generate **full-funnel marketing strategies** based on user input (e.g., target audience, budget, goals).
- Provide **ad copy variants** that adhere to each platform’s policies (e.g., character limits, prohibited content).
- Suggest **optimizations** for existing campaigns.

---

### **3. Settings**
The **Settings** tab allows users to:
- Manage **account details** (e.g., email, password).
- Connect **ad accounts** (e.g., Facebook Ads, Google Ads) for analytics and performance tracking.
- Customize **preferences** (e.g., default templates, design settings).

---

### **4. Scalability**
FunnelBrain AI is designed to be **modular**, allowing new features to be added over time, such as:
- Additional **ad channels** (e.g., TikTok, Pinterest).
- Advanced **AI features** (e.g., predictive analytics, dynamic ad optimization).
- **Collaboration tools** for teams and agencies.

---

## Key Features Summary
